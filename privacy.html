Privacy and data

How JWTechDev.ai handles user data right now.

This page explains the current data paths for the static site, Lemon Squeezy checkouts, free resources, and inquiry emails.

Current setup

Short version

JWTechDev.ai is currently a static website. Product purchases and free bundle access run through Lemon Squeezy. General inquiries open a structured email draft to jwtechdev@gmail.com. JWTechDev.ai is not currently running its own public lead database.

Checkout

Lemon Squeezy data

When you buy a digital product or request a free bundle through Lemon Squeezy, the information you enter into that checkout is handled by Lemon Squeezy. That may include your email address, order details, payment status, tax/compliance details where required, and download/access records.

JWTechDev.ai uses Lemon Squeezy so checkout and digital delivery can happen through a dedicated commerce provider instead of a custom payment backend.

Inquiries

Contact form data

The current inquiry form does not submit to a JWTechDev.ai database. If no approved form endpoint is active, the form opens your email app with a structured draft addressed to jwtechdev@gmail.com.

If you send that email, the information you include is stored in the receiving email mailbox and used to respond to your inquiry, evaluate fit, or route the request.

Not active

What is not currently active

  • No public JWTechDev.ai lead-capture backend is active.
  • No local CSV lead vault is receiving public website submissions.
  • No Google Sheet or Drive-based lead database is connected to the public site.
  • No Formspree endpoint is currently configured on the public inquiry form.
  • The site does not intentionally use browser localStorage or sessionStorage for lead data.
Use

How the data is used

Data is used to deliver requested resources, provide purchase access, respond to inquiries, evaluate brand partnership or speaking fit, and improve JWTechDev.ai educational offerings.

JWTechDev.ai does not sell personal inquiry data. If a future analytics, email, CRM, or lead-capture provider is added, this page should be updated before that system is treated as live.

Requests

Access, correction, or removal

For questions about data shared directly with JWTechDev.ai, email jwtechdev@gmail.com. For Lemon Squeezy checkout records, some account, receipt, tax, and compliance data may need to remain in Lemon Squeezy according to their policies and legal requirements.
