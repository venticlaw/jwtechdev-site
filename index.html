About me

Senior Manager, Cloud & Engineering who hangs out at the anime con.

I’ve spent more than a decade in tech — from racking servers in a closet to running cloud and engineering at Deloitte. Army veteran, cloud architect, and now I make videos about all of it.

My whole thing is this: complex concepts are not actually complex, they’re just badly explained. I take the gnarly stuff — Kubernetes, IAM, retrieval-augmented generation — and turn it into something you’d watch on your phone while waiting for the bus. With jokes. And usually one anime reference.

Off the channel I’m an AWS Community Builder, co-founder of Building Our Tech (BOT) — a non-profit focused on getting more underrepresented folks into cloud careers — and I live in Florida where the cloud is both a metaphor and a weather report.
