Creator Infrastructure

How can creators use static websites to avoid platform lock-in?

Creators avoid platform lock-in by owning a static website, keeping content portable, building an email list, and treating social platforms as distribution channels instead of headquarters.

Direct Answer

A static site gives creators a durable home base that is cheap to host, easy to mirror, simple to version, and not dependent on one algorithm.

The Ownership Stack

  • Static site: the public home.
  • Markdown archive: the portable source of truth.
  • Email list: the owned relationship.
  • Social channels: distribution, discovery, and feedback.
  • Local backups: resilience when tools or platforms change.

Why It Matters

Platform reach is useful, but it is rented attention. Your site and archive are where the work compounds.

Beginner Mistake To Avoid

Do not wait for a perfect custom platform. Start with a static page, a clear offer, a simple waitlist path, and a habit of saving reusable source files.
