Brand strategy

Two brands, two promises

Do not blend their audiences or voice. The shared system creates efficiency; the public identities remain distinct.

JWTechDev

Keep the expertise. Widen the entrance.

JWT remains focused on AWS, cloud, enterprise AI, agents, architecture, governance, identity, security, and career translation. Wider appeal comes from the hook, story, analogy, demonstration, and consequence, not from becoming a generic gadget-review brand.

  • Accessible curiosity at the top
  • Real architecture in the middle
  • Practical action at the end
  • Aligned speaking, workshop, content, and brand opportunities

Fix Your Sound

Turn earned experience into visible guidance.

Josh is a 20-year music veteran with international placements and an NCAA placement, now using his engineering background to help music makers diagnose problems, make better decisions, and finish music with confidence.

  • Veteran: lessons the tutorials leave out
  • Engineer: clearer diagnosis without replacing taste
  • Builder: honest tests, mistakes, and product development
  • Guide: education, teardowns, client journeys, and proof

Operating boundaries

Emily should move without chasing Josh.

Routine marketing execution belongs to Emily. Josh enters only where his voice, expertise, relationship, or approval is genuinely required.

Emily owns

  • Audience, search, competitor, and comment research
  • Editorial calendar, briefs, hooks, titles, and captions
  • Thumbnail and visual briefs
  • First review of long-form and short-form edits
  • Repurposing, distribution preparation, and scheduling after approval
  • Topic-matched brand research and contact discovery
  • First-draft outreach, follow-up tracking, and campaign reporting
  • Analytics, audience learning, and next-topic recommendations
  • FYS testimonial, beta, client-journey, and community packaging

Josh owns

  • Founder point of view and final brand strategy
  • On-camera recording and product demonstrations
  • Technical, placement, client, and employer-sensitive claims
  • Which brands and relationships are appropriate
  • Prices, offers, budgets, deliverables, and commercial commitments
  • Final approval until a narrower standing approval is documented
  • High-value sponsor, speaking, customer, and event conversations
