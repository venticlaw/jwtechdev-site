Presenter Script

60-90 second version

I am not saying every fly got a stimulus check. I am saying the conditions after COVID got real comfortable for an insect with no rent, no job, and 600 children.

Flies need three things: heat, moisture, and gross organic material. Society looked at that list and said, "Say less."

Exhibit A: they breed like bad decisions. In hot conditions, house flies can go from egg to adult in about a week. One female can lay hundreds of eggs. So when you say, "I saw a fly," congratulations. You saw a startup pitch.

Exhibit B: the buffet is elite. EPA says food is the single most common material sent to U.S. landfills, and food plus other organic materials make up a huge chunk of landfill waste. That is not trash. That is fly brunch with methane.

Exhibit C: COVID changed the trash math. More delivery, more packaging, more household waste pressure, more PPE and plastic waste. I am not saying COVID made flies. I am saying we made more fly-friendly mess.

Exhibit D: heat makes the schedule nastier. Warmer conditions can speed development and shift fly activity. Global warming is basically giving pest behavior a calendar invite.

So why are there so many f*cking flies? Heat, waste, food, moisture, fast reproduction, and nearby breeding sites. They are not invading. They are responding to market conditions. And apparently the market is disgusting.

Stay blessed. Stay screened. Cloud Gang.
