Privacy and data

How JWTechDev.ai handles user data right now.

This page explains the current data paths for the static site, free kit requests, and inquiry emails.

Current setup

Short version

JWTechDev.ai is currently a static website. Visitors can request the free Cloud Starter Kit, ask about speaking engagements, or inquire about social media asset creation. General inquiries open a structured email draft to jwtechdev@gmail.com.

Free kit

Free kit request data

When you request the Cloud Starter Kit, JWTechDev.ai asks for your name, email address, and optional context about what you are trying to learn.

That information is used to send or follow up about the requested resource and related JWTechDev.ai educational content.

Inquiries

Email inquiry data

When you submit or send an inquiry, the information you provide may include your name, email address, organization, phone number, interest area, project context, and notes. That information is used to understand the request and decide whether it fits JWTechDev.ai boundaries for speaking engagements or social media asset work.

Do not send confidential employer, client, production-system, regulated workload, security, compliance, or legal information through the inquiry path.

Boundaries

Public-site boundaries

Only share information that is appropriate for an independent educational brand to review.

JWTechDev.ai content is independent education. It is not professional consulting, legal, compliance, security, employer/client-specific, or production-system advice.

Contact

Questions

For questions about data shared directly with JWTechDev.ai, email jwtechdev@gmail.com.
