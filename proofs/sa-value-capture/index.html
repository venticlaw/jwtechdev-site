Interim operating plan

Make architecture value visible.

A direct but defensible plan for capturing solution architecture demand, hours, and influenced business impact while the dedicated role is still informal.

Executive frame

The current model hides material SA demand.

The goal is to quantify demand, capacity, and business impact for solution architecture support that is currently handled through side-channel requests. The data should help leadership decide whether the work needs a dedicated enablement role with clear ownership, sales attachment, and measurable outcomes.

01 Capture every meaningful assist

Log account or pursuit, requesting team, support type, hours, funding mechanism, revenue influence, stage, outcome, and confirmation owner.

Single source of truth
02 Validate weekly instead of chasing daily

Send a Friday confirmation to each involved team with approximate hours and business context. Corrections are due by Monday.

Low friction
03 Convert activity into a scorecard

Summarize hours, accounts supported, pursuits influenced, closed-won impact, funding influence, risks reduced, reusable assets, and duplicated work avoided.

  • Reusable assets: templates, agentic tools, playbooks, process improvements.

Launch note

Use this to introduce the tracker.

I am starting a lightweight tracking process for solution architecture support so we can quantify demand, capacity, and business impact. For any account or pursuit where I support solutioning, funding strategy, partner alignment, SOW shaping, or delivery architecture, I will track approximate hours and associated pipeline or revenue influence. This is not a chargeback model. It is a visibility model.

Weekly confirmation

Use this every Friday.

For this week, I have your team down for roughly X hours of solution architecture support tied to Y pursuit or account. Revenue or pipeline influence is currently estimated at Z. Please confirm or correct by EOD Monday so the monthly leadership view is accurate.

30-day output

The leadership readout should be blunt.

In the last month, informal solution architecture support covered X accounts, Y pursuits, Z hours, and N in influenced pipeline or revenue. This is currently handled as a side responsibility, which creates scale risk and poor visibility. A dedicated enablement role would turn this into a measurable growth function.

Tone guardrails

Be direct without sounding aggrieved.

  • Say "value capture" instead of "collecting hours."
  • Say "influenced pipeline and delivery leverage" instead of personal credit claims.
  • Keep the tracker approximate unless a formal timekeeping model exists.
  • Do not publish client names, confidential revenue, private deal details, or internal escalations.
  • Use the data to request capacity, ownership, and sales attachment.
