About me

Senior eng who hangs out at the anime con.

I’ve spent more than a decade in tech — from racking servers in a closet to architecting cloud platforms most of you have probably used without knowing it. Now I make videos.

My whole thing is this: complex concepts are not actually complex, they’re just badly explained. I take the gnarly stuff — Kubernetes, IAM, retrieval-augmented generation — and turn it into something you’d watch on your phone while waiting for the bus. With jokes. And usually one anime reference.

Off the channel I’m an AWS Community Builder, I lead a tech non-profit focused on getting more underrepresented folks into cloud careers, and I live in Florida where the cloud is both a metaphor and a weather report.
