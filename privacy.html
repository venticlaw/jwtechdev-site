Privacy and data

How JWTechDev.ai handles user data right now.

This page explains the current data paths for the static site, public resources, and inquiry emails.

Current setup

Short version

JWTechDev.ai is currently a static website. Public resources are available through the site, and commercial requests are limited to inquiry-only speaking or source-led technical brief conversations. General inquiries open a structured email draft to jwtechdev@gmail.com. JWTechDev.ai is not currently running its own public lead database, checkout flow, or digital product delivery system.

Checkout

No checkout or paid digital asset flow

JWTechDev.ai does not currently sell paid digital downloads on the site. There is no active JWTechDev.ai checkout, payment form, or automatic digital delivery flow on this website.

Speaking and source-led technical brief requests are inquiry-only and should be scoped before any commitment is made.

Inquiries

Email inquiry data

When you submit or send an inquiry, the information you provide may include your name, email address, organization, phone number, interest area, project context, and notes. That information is used to understand the request and decide whether it fits JWTechDev.ai boundaries.

Do not send confidential employer, client, production-system, regulated workload, security, compliance, or legal information through the inquiry path.

Boundaries

What JWTechDev.ai does not collect here

This site does not collect payment information, run a custom account system, host a public lead database, or provide automatic paid file delivery.

JWTechDev.ai content is independent education. It is not professional consulting, legal, compliance, security, employer/client-specific, or production-system advice.

Contact

Questions

For questions about data shared directly with JWTechDev.ai, email jwtechdev@gmail.com. If you previously used a third-party checkout before the site changed, any historical account, receipt, tax, or compliance records remain subject to that provider's policies and legal requirements.
